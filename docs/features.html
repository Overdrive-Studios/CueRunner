Features

What CueRunner does, grouped by what you'd use it for.

Cues

Audio cues

Each cue has its own fade in, fade out, loop and output routing.

Follow and continue

Start the next cue when one ends, or straight away.

Countdown

A timer you can start, stop and reset for the start of a show.

Spotify cues

Play Spotify tracks and playlists from a link. Needs a Spotify Premium account.

Soundboard and radio

64 pads

Four banks of sixteen. Pads play straight away, separate from the cue list.

Ducking

Music drops to a set level while the talkback mic is open.

Venue radio

Plays a music folder with idents between tracks.

Audio

Talkback

Auto gain, gate, compressor, limiter and feedback suppression.

Recorder

Records up to 48 tracks to 24-bit WAV files.

Routing

Send any cue or pad to the outputs you choose.

Dante and ASIO

Works with Dante Virtual Soundcard, ASIO interfaces and USB audio.

Control

Consoles

Scene recall, mutes and fader levels for Allen & Heath SQ, dLive, Avantis and Qu-5/6/7.

MIDI

Trigger GO, STOP, PAUSE and PANIC from a MIDI controller, with your own mapping.

Phone remote

A browser page on your network, locked by a PIN.

Stream Deck

A plugin that sends GO, STOP, cue and pad commands to the phone remote.

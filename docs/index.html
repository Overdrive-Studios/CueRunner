What it does

Cue list

Audio cues with fades in and out, loops, follow and continue, and a countdown.

Soundboard

64 pads in four banks, with ducking under the talkback mic.

Venue radio

Plays a music folder with idents between tracks, routed to any outputs.

Talkback

Mic chain with auto gain, gate, compressor, limiter and feedback suppression.

Recorder

48-track recording to 24-bit WAV files.

Consoles

Allen & Heath SQ, dLive, Avantis and Qu-5/6/7 over the network.

Phone remote and Stream Deck

Control the show from a phone browser behind a PIN, or from a Stream Deck.

Spotify

Play Spotify tracks and playlists as cues, with a Spotify Premium account.

Dante and ASIO

Works with Dante Virtual Soundcard, ASIO interfaces and USB audio.

Install

Mac

  1. Click Download for Mac and open the CueRunner.dmg file.
  2. Drag CueRunner into your Applications folder.
  3. The first time, right-click CueRunner in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again.

Windows

  1. Click Download for Windows and run CueRunner.exe.
  2. If Windows shows a warning, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Updates

Once installed, open Help > Check for Updates inside CueRunner to get new versions. You won’t need to come back here.

Features

Everything a show needs

Cue list

Fades in and out, loops, follow and continue, hotkeys and a countdown.

Soundboard

64 pads in four banks, with ducking under the talkback mic.

Venue radio

Shuffled music from a folder, with idents between tracks.

Consoles

Allen & Heath SQ, dLive, Avantis and Qu-5/6/7 over the network.

Phone and Stream Deck

Run the show from a phone browser behind a PIN, or from a Stream Deck.

Recorder and Spotify

Record up to 48 tracks, and play Spotify tracks as cues.
